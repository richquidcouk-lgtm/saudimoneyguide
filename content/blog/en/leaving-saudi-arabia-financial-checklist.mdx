## Why the order matters

Doing this out of sequence creates real friction:

- Settling credit obligations *before* you know your final gratuity figure can mean paying from the wrong pool of money, when the gratuity itself might have covered it.
- Deciding on your bank account *after* your Iqama is cancelled can be too late — some account actions require an active residency status to process.
- Moving money home in a rush on your final week, rather than planning the transfer, is exactly how people end up accepting a bad exchange rate on their largest transfer of the year.

## What doesn't require action

- **Your SIMAH file** isn't something you "close" — it simply stops accumulating new Saudi activity. It's not relevant to future credit applications outside Saudi Arabia, which run on entirely separate credit bureaus.
- **Zakat and investment holdings**, if you have any through a Saudi platform, follow that platform's own account-closure or transfer-out process — contact them directly well before your exit date, since liquidating investments can take longer than a simple bank transfer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long before I leave should I start this checklist?**
Start confirming your gratuity figure and bank account situation at least a month out — both can involve back-and-forth with HR or your bank that doesn't resolve in a single conversation.

**Does leaving Saudi Arabia affect my credit history if I return later?**
Your SIMAH file reflects your history up to your departure; if you return and resume financial activity, that historical record is generally still there, though how relevant older history is to a fresh application is something to confirm with SIMAH or a lender directly.

**What happens if I still owe money on a loan or credit card when I leave?**
This should be settled before departure — leaving with an unresolved balance is a serious issue, not something that's simply left behind, and can affect your ability to return or obtain financial products in Saudi Arabia in the future.

**Can I keep a Saudi bank account open after I leave permanently?**
This depends on the specific bank and account type — some allow it under certain conditions, others require closure once your residency ends. Confirm directly with your bank rather than assuming.

## Summary

Confirm your final settlement figure, check your GOSI status, clear any outstanding credit, decide on your bank account, then move your remaining money home through a licensed channel — in that order. Most of the friction people hit when leaving Saudi Arabia comes from doing these out of sequence or leaving them to the final week.
