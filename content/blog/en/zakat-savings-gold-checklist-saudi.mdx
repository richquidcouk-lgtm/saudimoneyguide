A Zakat calculator is only as useful as the asset information entered into it. Before calculating, organize the balances, quantities and valuation dates you will use. This reduces missed accounts, duplicated amounts and results based on an outdated gold price.

## Prepare the records first

Collect cash balances, relevant gold or silver quantities, investment statements and information about liabilities you want assessed. [ZATCA’s individual Zakaty service](https://www.zatca.gov.sa/ar/eServices/Pages/eservices-306.aspx) distinguishes a short cash calculator from a detailed calculator for different asset categories. Use the appropriate route rather than putting unlike assets into one unexplained total.

## Keep ownership and valuation clear

Make a list of what you own, its relevant value and the evidence supporting that value. If you move money between your own accounts, avoid counting both the outgoing and incoming amount as separate wealth. For foreign-currency balances, use a consistent valuation approach and keep a record of the rate used.

| Record | What to note |
| --- | --- |
| Cash and bank accounts | Balance and valuation date |
| Gold | Weight, purity and price basis |
| Investments | Asset type, quantity and relevant statement |
| Potential deductions | Nature, timing and reason for assessment |

This is a preparation checklist, not a ruling that every listed item is treated the same way or that every liability is deductible.

## An arithmetic example, after eligibility is established

If the correctly assessed Zakat base is SAR 40,000 and the applicable calculation uses 2.5%, multiplication gives SAR 1,000. That example does not establish whether your assets meet the relevant conditions, how a particular holding period is treated or which deductions are allowed. Resolve those questions before treating the arithmetic as an amount due.

## Gold-price defaults need checking

A stored example price is not a live quote. Check purity and the unit used: a per-gram price and a total jewelry value are not interchangeable. [ZATCA’s Zakaty user guide](https://www.zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/ZakatyManual.pdf) describes its linked price information and support for multiple asset types. Consult the official service or qualified guidance for your actual assessment.

Use our [Zakat calculator](/tools/zakat-calculator) as a planning estimate with its stated assumptions. Do not assume a generic cash-and-gold formula resolves investment structures or other complex holdings. Keep the date, inputs and final assessment together for next year’s review.

## Avoid common record errors

Check for forgotten savings accounts, assets counted twice and values taken from different dates without explanation. If you have already paid an amount, record the date and what it related to rather than simply subtracting it from a new calculation. Keep sensitive account records private.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I use last year’s gold price?**
Use a valuation appropriate to the assessment date and the asset’s purity, not an old illustrative default.

**Is every debt deductible?**
Do not assume that. The nature of the liability and applicable assessment need to be considered.

**Does multiplying savings by 2.5% always give the answer?**
Only after the relevant base and conditions have been correctly established.
