No Keyword Planner, Search Console or paid keyword-volume dataset was available. Priorities below are editorial judgments, not measured search-volume rankings. No page can guarantee rankings or cover every search query.

## Remaining coverage to validate and develop

1. Current product comparisons: salary-transfer/no-transfer personal finance, bank accounts for residents, cards/cashback caps and annual fees. Obtain dated provider disclosure sheets before ranking products.
2. City budgets: Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam/Khobar by household type, with current rent and transport evidence. Avoid duplicate city pages without distinct data.
3. Housing: Sakani eligibility, first-home costs, rent-versus-buy, real-estate transaction tax, early settlement and variable-rate scenarios. Source current rules.
4. Employment: overtime, leave pay, wage disputes, job-loss benefits, dependant costs and freelancer obligations. Separate nationals/residents where eligibility differs.
5. Insurance: Takaful, motor claim excesses, no-claims discounts, family health coverage and network comparisons. Maintain official policy references.
6. Saving/investing: emergency-fund accounts, Saudi ETFs/funds, Sukuk structures, investment eligibility for residents, retirement scenarios and detailed Zakat cases.
7. Payments: SADAD, IBAN, transfer limits, mada versus credit, wallet eligibility, BNPL obligations and refunds. Avoid unsupported provider fee or approval claims.
8. Dedicated tools: household budget, live-data remittance comparison, balloon payments, rent-versus-buy, card rewards and retirement planning. Existing tools remain at 12; related-reading links do not implement missing calculation features.

## Measurement loop

Use verified Search Console exports segmented by Saudi Arabia and language to record query, landing page, clicks, impressions, CTR and position. Group equivalent intents and choose one primary page for each. Use Keyword Planner or a licensed keyword source for Saudi search-volume estimates, recording source and date. Prioritize evidenced gaps and refresh source-sensitive pages before expanding near-duplicates. Track publication, substantive updates, sources and actual reviewer details separately.
